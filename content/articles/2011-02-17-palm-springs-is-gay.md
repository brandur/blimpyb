# Palm Springs is Gay

No really, check out the guide in the tourist info center, or even their website. I liked the vibe of the place with everything moving a lot slower than LA, it’s a lot smaller than you’d think, I’m just saying that if I liked dudes I would have stuck around longer.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** The town is a strange mix of upscale, and not; **alt:** Very Posh)

Halfway through my typical mid-afternoon stroll through a new town I went to my standard fall back, looking at the Happy Hour app on my phone (I made the mistake of checking recently and found out I use this app more than any other). Seeing a place called Hunter’s with a strip of three other bars on Arenas Road, I turned the corner and immediately knew what I was getting myself into. Rainbow flags everywhere, and sure, this is California, you’ll see them in regular places just to show the business isn’t discriminating to customers, but when they’re on every store on the block it’s a bit more apparent. It’s also the first place I saw a US flag with the stars and stripes replaced with a rainbow, I can’t wait to see what people in Oklahoma have to think about that.

Sidling up to the bar to order my $1.50 Bud Light, I am dismayed to find Hunter’s doesn’t have a kitchen, so my visit will be brief. Anxious to keep my business, the bartender assures me the place will be hopping in an hour once people start getting off work, and they open the dance floor early because it’s bear night. He calls me “sweetheart” a few times during the conversation, I eat some free chips and salsa on the patio and am off.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Interesting architecture; **alt:** I hope Frank Sinatra banked here)

Not 10 minutes later some 60 year old Mexican woman stops me on the street, tells me she likes the way I dress, that she is a widow and I should come back to her place. Politely declining because she was about 5’3”, over 200 lbs and missing a few teeth, I let my Happy Hour app lead me to another spot, this time with food guaranteed as one of the specials is free appetizers. Somewhere in the midst of disengaging my Mexican lady friend she also informs me that her daughter is an American citizen and single, but the call of free appies is much stronger.

O’Learys is a prettied up dive bar, but the second I walk in a large steaming tray of huge, saucy buffalo wings is placed at a table with napkins and ranch sauce. The woman behind the bar tells me to help myself after serving me a $2 PRB pint (I paid $4 for a bottle in LA) and tells me she is from Vancouver Island after getting my TD Canada Trust credit card to open up a tab. A few minutes later another older lady in the bar starts chatting with me because of my camera (it’s a great conversation starter with complete strangers) and tells me she is from Vancouver Island. Surprised, I start telling her that I just…

“Yup, that’s my sister! We always come here on Friday night, she gives us free drinks!” Ahhh, to be in a small town again.
