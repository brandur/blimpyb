# Wide Angle Madness

So I’ve been holding out on all my good content pretending I’m too busy. Well, I actually am too busy, but never too busy to take some more dope photos.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Back to my old HDR tricks; **alt:** HDR under the pier)

I was lucky enough at Christmas this year to get a Sigma 8-16 lens for my Canon T2i, and while I’ve had limited time to test it out, so far the results are absolutely outstanding. One of these “side projects” I keep talking about is actually a site dedicated to pimping the lens where I can have my commercial slant but still stay true to my passions of photography without completely selling out. It’s not ready yet which is why I was holding off on letting the cat out of the bag, but I have enough sample shots now there’s no point depriving everyone. Plus, I can always edit this post, make a few more and self-promote and backlink the sh!t out of myself…but that’s another story for another day.

In the meantime, these shots were all taken within a 20 minute walk of where I’m staying in beautiful Pacific Beach in San Diego California. It’s been 70 degrees F and sunny for a few weeks now, and I’m obviously not too disappointed about my plan for the last 8 or so months to “winter” here, as I believe the photos will illustrate.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Perhaps more the subject than the shot; **alt:** Lotus Exige)

Some of you may have had a chance to check out my [Canon extension tube](https://web.archive.org/web/20110204181613/http://blimpyb.com/photography/canon-extension-tube/3362/) work in a post last week, this one is just another trick in the lens bag. Almost every shot from my trip to date was taken with my Sigma 30mm prime lens, but now that I’ve got the wide angle, it opens up a whole new world. Without getting overly technical, 30mm versus 8mm means I can fit 375% more subject in each frame. While it seems like this would be best for landscape photography, I’m finding it’s actually best for capturing and amplifying a subject in the foreground pitted against an expansive background. But, I haven’t had enough chance to test out all the possible combinations, so once I get back on the road again on Thursday, the experimentation across America should commence once again.

In the meantime, check [the gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20110204181613/http://blimpyb.com/gallery/san-diego-wide-angle/).
