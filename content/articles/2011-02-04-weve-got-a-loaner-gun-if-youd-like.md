# We’ve got a loaner gun if you’d like?

What a difference 40 miles make. After leaving Bessie parked in the Pacific Beach neighbourhood of San Diego with the doors unlocked for weeks, I departed north for Newport Beach with a pit stop for a tune up at Gary’s RV in Oceanside. It’s the classic case where someone who knows their blood pressure is giving them dizzy spells will postpone going to the doctor because they can still walk and breathe. Such is the case with an RV I have driven all the way from Canada to the Mexican border the entire time unable to cut the engine while in Park due to it continuing to fire and shoot black smoke everywhere.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Bessie's equivalent of triple bypass surgery; **alt:** Bessie's innerds)

To hear that the carburetor was “completely fucked” (Gary’s words, not mine) was no surprise, nor the fact I’d have to sleep in the parking lot overnight. The idea of that seemed downright comforting after a friend I met in San Diego who goes by the name “Crazy Scott” decided to stop by Bessie my last night sometime around 11 PM with a flashlight, while banging on the doors and windows yelling “this is the SDPD, open up!” I thought it was quite smart of me to hide under the covers and pretend the RV was uninhabited…well, I still think that was a good choice after hearing the neighbours on the street the night earlier proclaiming during one of my late night techno parties “not only does he keep parking here, but he has to rub it in our face!”

Every mechanic I’ve met so far is somehow amazed that Bessie’s held together as well as she has, and Gary could tell from the seals that my carburetor had never been rebuilt, and he swore he’d never seen one last more than a dozen years. So out it goes, gets soaked in some solvent overnight, gets some new gaskets and fittings in the morning, and $10 says I’ll make the $400 back on fuel economy alone by the time my trip is done. Well, I’m actually working on a sales pitch for Gary to see if he’d like his store ranked #1 in Google, but he already told me he “fucking hates computers” so it might be a tough sell.

Having been told the shop closed at 5 PM sharp, I returned at 4:45 to get the keys to the overnight gate to find Gary and a friend of his in the office watching some DVD on Fords of the Fifties. I was immediately pushed out a chair, given a Diet Pepsi and invited to join what they lovingly referred to as “car porn”. After deciphering that I was over 21, Gary offered me some of the whiskey he and his friend were drinking, before his friend shouted “no, there’s not enough!” and poured himself another two finger glass.

After finishing the movie over an hour later and learning a great deal about overhead valves, big blocks, that the grill on an Edsel “looks like a vagina” and that mechanics over the age of 50 are all generally great guys because they love what they do so much, I told Gary I was going to go back up towards Grubby’s where he’d pointed me for lunch earlier.

“Kid, I wouldn’t head up that direction, Oceanside’s a different place at night.”

Seeing the quizzical look on my face, mainly because Grubby’s was under 2 blocks away and I would be sleeping here, he offered me a bit of American reassurance.

“We’ve got a loaner gun if you’d like?”
