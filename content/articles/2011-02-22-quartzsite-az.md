# Just Another Day at the Office in Quartzsite AZ

What bothers me is that I actually have a plan _and_ I’m executing it. Two weeks ago I said I needed to go park in the middle of the desert away from any distractions and be productive, and here I am. Even at the time I wavered, contemplating robbing banks or selling drugs as I’ve always joked, but unfortunately no. For five days I’ve been camped out on BLM lands 6 miles south of Quartzsite AZ…working.

I haven’t moved more than 10 yards in that timeframe, I haven’t spoken to a soul, I certainly haven’t showered or shaved. I eat and drink the food and water I packed in, I harvest the energy of the sun to power my laptop tethered to my phone to access the internet to hock various products on various websites in what are but the crude, malnourished saplings of “internet business”. It’s a necessary evil, but being the first time I’ve done such “work” for personal commercial gain in oh, a year, it obviously makes me hearken back to the good ol’ days which I jokingly refer to with new acquaintances I meet on the road as “when I used to be a normal person”. As you can well imagine, I take stock of what lines illicit the best response after telling my story about a hundred times, and that one ranks quite highly.

Problem is, the old necessary evil had no beginning or end, no means or justification. This evil is mine and mine alone, so I can just put it on pause, drive to from Quartzsite AZ to Phoenix AZ and party. But Phoenix scares me, all I can picture is old people, mega malls, firearms and Christianity. It is but two hours away and I will likely drive there Thursday to watch my NASCAR and spring training baseball (as also foreshadowed), then turn around and return to the desert.

Realizations, several of them occur rapid fire now that I have nothing but time to think, and I welcome it. One, I need but $12000 a year to maintain this lifestyle. Self-employed or not, think what amount of effort you would need to procure that amount of money yourself. Hopefully the answer is minimal. Two, $12000 a year is right on the so-called poverty line, yet in my mind I live like a king. Three, I once had visions, aspirations that I could yell these things at people that they could do it too themselves and be free, to which I now fully understand they would respond “free to live in a van, alone in the middle of the desert?” And they would be correct. Perhaps I am uniquely adapted, or perhaps it is a grotesque mutation as they are often one in the same.

You know what, scratch that, you should probably quit your job and be free to live in a van in the middle of the desert. At least I didn’t yell that.
