# You Can Check Arkansas Off The List

After spending the last month in Memphis, it apparently became time for a brief weekend saunter to a campground outside of town for a lil’ break and to make sure Bessie still works, so off I went the nearest State Park an hour away in Arkansas. The fridge broke, but other than that the old girl is still holding up fine…

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** My fire poker, which is actually the size of my pinky; **alt:** Rusty rod)

With Forrest City being the town closest to the park (and possessing a Walmart for supplies…hell, it’s Arkansas, Walmart is a landmark), the name immediately conjured up images of oak trees and owls nestled around pristine lakes and rivers. While this may indeed by the case, it’s a funny thing how one extra R in Forrest changes the meaning greatly. Yes, that would be Nathan Bedford Forrest, the Civil War general and founding member of the KKK, there’s a giant statue of him in Nashville too, but you know, this is the South, so they’re clearly only revering his Civil War tactical mastery and nothing else and if you say any different you better shut your mouth. I’m not sure if the fact there’s a majority black population is Forrest City is funny, ironic or something I shouldn’t be talking about.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Back at the zoo in Memphis; **alt:** Polar bears chilling out)

I had seen Arkansas more than once as I trudged up the Mississippi Delta in November, but honestly had no intention of ever crossing the river. I won’t be visiting any major cities or interacting with many locals in the forest, so I’ll have to reserve judgement, but…it seems nice? Bill Clinton is from here, he’s a man. And as a random aside, over the last four weeks I have met two different people who mentioned they had visited all 50 states. Why anyone would do that is beyond me (apologies to South Dakota and Nebraska) and considering I’ve only been to 13 states in the last year and a half, I don’t want to be driving around until 2017 just to say I saw Mt. Rushmore. Or do I…?

Regardless, to kill time at the campground I busted out the old extension tubes for the camera lens and took a few macro shots, and tagged on some from the Memphis zoo to make [the gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20130918001641/http://blimpyb.com/gallery/arkansas/).
