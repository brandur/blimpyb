# Calgary Stampede 2011: Baby Steps

How sad is this, I could have got in no line, no cover at Cowboys tonight and decided to save it for another day!

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Why does this look so familiar?; **alt:** Equilibrium Circus 1)

I haven’t been in this much sensory overload wandering the Stampede grounds since I watched Transformers 3, and that’s saying a lot, that was almost aneurism territory. I figured rather than waking up early for the pre-parade breakfast and puking on the Marshall’s shoes, I might actually just ease into this whole thing rather than emptying my clip on day 1.

I’m already pretty in with the Stampede Queen and Princesses having my second line after meeting them be “I like my hat bent wrong, I’m not taking it off for you to fix because I haven’t showered today.” In Amarillo Texas that would come across as smooth, I still need to readjust. With one of my 20 events being “How to shop for a hat and boots” coupled with my clothing store gift certificate, maybe I can get Eliza Doolittle on errrbody and pretty myself up a touch. Maybe.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Wait, that's my name; **alt:** Equilibrium Circus 2)

But yeah, baby steps. I’m still too frightened by the horrific Stampede powers I posses to use them for evil _or_ good. During my backstage tour with the Young Canadians before the Grandstand Show, one location in the story was described to me as the Garden of Imagination. I later referred to it as Imaginationland…which is NOT the same thing. Thank God nobody coordinating the event watches much South Park.

And seriously, I tweeted about it, but the scene with the acrobat was as priceless as it gets. I was sitting in the Grandstand Show Committee trailer while they hammered out the last details of the first dress rehearsal and in walked a tall long-haired guy asking if he could get into Nashville North after the show and was told they’d really cracked down on that the last few years, which I can attest to not even being able to jump line as the Ultimate Intern. I always tell people I’m terrible at names but amazing with faces, so after a minute I chimed in “hey, are you an acrobat? Did you perform on Granville Island last summer?” Sort of shrugging it off but still excited I recognized him, he piped back “yeah, glad you saw it, lemme go get you these promotion cards we have!”

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Ohhhh, that pic from 11 months ago!; **alt:** Don't try this at home)

So he comes back and gives me a card and I’m just staring at it continuously thinking that looks EXACTLY like the shot I took on Granville Island. Then I flip over the card and it has photography credits from Blimpy Backgammon. Apparently I’ve seen so much in the last year I completely forgot e-mailing him my pics and providing the quotable on the back “Equilibrium Circus is ridiculously talented!” At least I still stand by it, because dammit, they are, expect Twitter updates on shows they plan to do in town, totally worth checking out.
