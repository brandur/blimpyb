# Ultimate Calgary Stampede Intern

Every day until June 17th vote for Blimpy as the Ultimate [Calgary Stampede Intern](https://web.archive.org/web/20111213041757/http://wildfireapp.com/website/6/contests/121933/voteable_entries/23555318?ogn=website "Calgary Stampede Intern")! It’s a fantastic contest being run by the Calgary Stampede and if I win I get to live on the grounds for 10 days in my own trailer with 24/7 backstage access to blog about all kinds of fun and ridiculous stuff! You can also watch my [Calgary Stampede Intern](https://web.archive.org/web/20111213041757/http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrua0Zq6q3A "Calgary Stampede Ultimate Intern") video on YouTube, but please make sure to [click the link to vote every day until June 17th](https://web.archive.org/web/20111213041757/http://wildfireapp.com/website/6/contests/121933/voteable_entries/23555318?ogn=website).

[![Calgary Stampede Intern](https://web.archive.org/web/20111213041757im_/http://blimpyb.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/stampede3-383x600.jpg "Calgary Stampede Intern")](https://web.archive.org/web/20111213041757/http://blimpyb.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/06/stampede3.jpg)The video itself goes through all the reasons why I think I’m qualified to show everyone a rip-roaring good time and I really appreciate everyone’s support. Plus, you’ll be paid back in spades if I’m lucky enough to win as I promise it will be 10 days of absolute hilarity. As the Ultimate Calgary Stampede Intern I’ll be able to cover 20 different events at the Stampede on an iPhone 4 while drafting up videos, blog posts and tweets.

Also, I’m really enjoying the chance to improve in filming and editing videos, so just think what kind of a show I can come up with when the grounds are full of people! As you can see from the one lady at the end of my video, the Calgary Stampede seems to attract lots of excitable characters, so mix in the rodeo, some BBQ and sunny July heat and it’s guaranteed to be a great ride.

Thanks again to everyone for following Blimpy and I’d be forever grateful if you could pass the link along to anyone you can think of!
