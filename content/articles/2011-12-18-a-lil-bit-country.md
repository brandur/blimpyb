# A lil’ bit Country

“I love your hat, where’d you get it?” the waitress asked of the pink-sequined Santa hat I was wearing as we ate sushi.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** The hat repetoir grows; **alt:** Fushcia hat...my "what is happenning?!?!" face)

“I found it on the floor last night at an ugly sweater party at Blue Bar. Frosty the snow man was DJing.” I still think it has some potential to become a seasonal pimp hat (it’s more fuchsia than pink), but her quizzical eye wrinkle at the floor bit made me realize the line needs some refining. Good thing I didn’t tell her about the forgotten soldier beer I randomly picked off the bar and drank later in the night, it was still three quarters full and coldish, so basically the deal of the night.

Rewinding, I have been in Nashville for over a week now, sorry I skipped over my post on Memphis. I think that’s because the most poignant observations I had were the “6 block rule” that no matter how safe you are, walk six blocks and you aren’t, that Terrence Howard’s accent in Hustle and Flow was way off but Ludacris had it bang on and that 11 AM is a really busy time at the convenience store because there will be 4 people ahead of you buying 40s. Crazy good weed though, nothing like rolling around with my 6’8” buddy Tre at 2 AM on a Friday rockin some “strawberry cough” reminiscing about his days playing college ball in Arkansas.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** OK, the DJ wasn't country; **alt:** I told you Frosty the Snowman was DJing)

Up here in Music City, I’ve been staying with one of the most talented keyboardists you’ve ever met, so far hitting up a few recording studios, the Hammond repair shop, meeting a wide variety of musicians and song-writers, watching my Calgary Flames lose because they actually have a hockey team here, and just being generally busy. ‘tis the season I suppose.

I kinda feel bad having [a whole gallery of phone camera shots](https://web.archive.org/web/20120207015957/http://blimpyb.com/gallery/nashville/), but I’ve been coming across all kinds of stuff. Apparently the thing to do is build a recording studio in your own basement so you don’t have to book time and can overdub tracks in your underwear and still get paid, but watching someone putting 2-inch stops under the new door frame in their severely curved/sunken 130 year old home makes me laugh at a new adage I have heard about recording studios. They’re like a boat, a lot more fun when you’ve got a buddy who owns one.
