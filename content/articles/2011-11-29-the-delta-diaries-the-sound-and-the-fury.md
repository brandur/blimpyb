# The Delta Diaries: The Sound and the Fury

OK, so Oxford isn’t actually in the Delta as evidenced by the fact there are hills, but it’s close enough, and I absolutely adore alliteration. Here’s [the gallery](https://web.archive.org/web/20130606173932/http://blimpyb.com/gallery/oxford-ms/).

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** One bourbon, one scotch, one Faulkner; **alt:** One for my homies)

Home to all things William Faulkner and the legendary university Ole’ Miss, Oxford sticks out like a sore thumb versus the rest of northern Mississippi. A rich, white, antebellum thumb steeped in tradition to the point many would tell you it’s the absolute epicentre of “the South”, and I would have a tough time arguing against that. Students attend their football games in khaki suits and summer dresses and the only time you can buy alcohol on a Sunday is if Ole’ Miss had a home game the day before, which unfortunately wasn’t the case the Sunday I rolled in.

It also happens to be the school James Meredith enrolled at in 1962, forcing Kennedy to send in the National Guard to stop the riots with him being their first black student. He was subsequently shot on a march from Memphis to Jackson in 1966, but survived, although I’m told no one showed up to see him speak on the 40th anniversary of his enrolment, well, no one who was white. &nbsp;Tough life being a hero.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Gorgeous town, tons of history; **alt:** See, Oxford is antebellum)

Another great Ole’ Miss story was them deciding to retire their mascot Colonel Rebel in 2003 because it was deemed to be racist due to his costume as a plantation owner. However, when your team name is the Rebels, there’s a memorial to all who died fighting the Union in the center of campus and your state flag still has the Confederate flag on it, who’s kidding who and what does it matter? Apparently all merchandize bearing the Colonel’s likeness stopped being sold by the school, but some local business/historical folks started the [Colonel Reb Foundation](https://web.archive.org/web/20130606173932/http://www.saveolemiss.com/), secured rights to his likeness and are now able to profit off him instead.

There was a long, drawn-out fight/vote/what-have-you to try and name a replacement and they somehow arrived at the Rebel Black Bear, even though there isn’t a sizeable population of black bears in this region of Mississippi and he has yet to make an appearance at a game. At a local bar named Roosters ($2 pitchers on Monday, 3 stories, 3 bars in one…ugh it still hurts) they sell t-shirts with the Colonel flipping the bird and the caption “Bear This!” that sell by the thousands. So like I said originally, what was the point? The Washington Redskins is still a million times worse when you think about it.

_(missing image)_ (**caption:** Not good RV weather; **alt:** Ugh, why the hell is there snow here?!?!)

My month in Mississippi is now drawing to a close as I head towards Memphis, but I must say the Delta has without a doubt been the most memorable stop of my entire journey. Not that I would recommend it for everyone, but I have learned an incredible amount about society past and present, which I think was part of my adventure’s goal, along with the striking scenery this area has. Unfortunately even in the South they get a little something called winter, which I was not expecting, so after Nashville I’m pretty sure I’ll be making a bee line for Florida.
