2010: A Year in Review

Blimpy On December - 29 - 2010

Having already covered the beer highlights of the year, here’s a list of some of my favourite and least favourite travel adventures and destinations of 2010.

Favourite Place: Vancouver

What's not to like?

There’s a bunch of reasons I stayed in Van longer than anywhere else.  Beautiful summer weather, beaches and the ocean, a bunch of my good friends, and heck, being Canadian I may as well be biased and choose somewhere from my motherland.  But beyond that, if forced to pick Vancouver is easily the #1 spot I would return to live in.  It’s got great variety and culture, a huge music scene and more cheap sushi than you can shake a stick at.

Least Favourite Place: Westport

Whatever you do, don’t ever go here.  Well, that is unless you like run-ins with crack heads and night creatures, broken down closed stores, abandoned lots and some of the worst vibes on the entire West coast.

Place I Wish I’d Stayed More: Santa Cruz

A college town with an incredibly chill surfer and pot grower vibe, nestled between gorgeous scenic cliffs on the ocean and the mountains, and housing one of my favourite microbreweries, three days here definitely wasn’t enough.  While Monterey an hour south is more visually stunning with more tourist attractions, it lacks the true character and type of scene suited to a 27 year old van-dweller found in Santa Cruz.

Place I Wish I’d Stayed Less: San Francisco

Nice place to visit, wouldn't want to live here

More than anything I think it’s just a case of “don’t believe the hype”.  I probably did more fun things here than anywhere else (Underworld concert, Giants winning the World Series, Raider Nation) but with it being talked up incessantly to me for months, I guess I figured I’d gain the powers to shoot rainbows out my ass and fountains would spout wine.  I think it’s a very nice place to do touristy things, make a list of what you want to do, stop in for 4 days, see them all, then get out.  But after the 2+ weeks I was there I felt nothing but claustrophobic and weary.  All the streets are lined with 6 story apartment buildings and coupled with the steep hills throughout town you can’t really see where you’re coming or going, but will be incessantly told how interesting and unique everything is as though you can’t possibly hope to see or understand it being a foreigner…even though most everyone in the city isn’t from there either.

Best Way To Spend $30: A Raiders Game

Raaaaaaaaiderrrrrrrrs

I realized the lasting effect of going to my first NFL game at the Oakland-Alameda County Coliseum when I was watching football with a friend in LA weeks later and inadvertently shouted “KILL THEM!!” at the television.  The old Blimpy never thought of sporting events as death matches, now I want blood.  The seething energy and vicious chants of the Raider Nation really do leave a lasting impression and I certainly will be picking up a Raiders hat at some point soon, just to be one of those assholes in a Raiders hat across the whole country.  Honestly, there’s a reason they sell more merchandise than any team in the NFL.

Worst Way To Spend $30: A concert at Seattle’s Baltic Room

The fact that I listen to Starkey about 10% as much as before I saw him live really isn’t his fault, 90% of the blame lies on the worst concert venue I’ve ever been at.  When you’re going to see an electronic artist who’s act is mostly sub-bass and ultra-treble trickery and neither are coming out of the speakers properly, let down is putting it lightly.  Worse still is when the artist has to balance their time pumping up the crowd with making the speakers actually work properly and giving the in house sound techs dirty looks because them cooling off the overheating equipment by waving coasters clearly isn’t working.  The place is really only suited for an unplugged acoustic rock show, or for not bothering to go to at all.
