Nelson: Radical Self-Expressionists or Undesirables?

Blimpy On July - 11 - 2010

It might sound like a strange thing to say, but Nelson feels like a town at war.  For those of you not from Western Canada, tucked between the steep slopes and the lake of the Kootenay valley in the Rockies, over 5 hours from Calgary or Vancouver, the 10,000 person town of Nelson is known as the pot capital of Canada.  25% of all weed smuggled to the US originates from this area.  And so as you might imagine, it has a reputation for attracting a certain type of people.  Like those who quit their jobs, live in a van and like smoking joints.

As long as you're comfortable...

Within 5 minutes of rolling into my first camp spot behind the Safeway, I saw a van technicolour graffitied more than I would have done up Bessie given the chance, and out of it pouring a lot of smoke and a lot of dreadlocks.  This was stereotypical enough to be expected, as I suppose in a way was the fellow I found passed out next to my van at 2:30 AM.  Or perhaps the free yerba mate being given out at one of the vegan cafes the next morning, or the guy practicing long sword in the middle of a soccer field on my way to the beach that afternoon, or the girl who’d shaved part of her eyebrows and slapped some sort of leopard tattoo up there, or…

None of this is really a problem…well, the last one, what’s your opening line “so you like cats?”…but I did come across one guy who kicked his soccer ball past me on the sidewalk so I would briefly join him, we passed it back and forth, I watched him go on ahead and play the same game with a random lady, this time absent-mindedly kicking it into an intersection full of traffic, her barely stopping herself.  Death by lackadaisical hippie?

They must serve food that's EXTREME...ly uncooked. D'oh.

Suddenly the fact dogs, skateboards, hand drums and hacky sacks all are or were banned from downtown at some point doesn’t seem nearly as crotchety.  At the same time, it’s still a free country.  But, for all the pot smoking 20-somethings that like to shred, or disillusioned urban liberals, there’s still a whole whack of lifers, regular old people who own fruit stands and have watched their town change drastically and curse under their breath as they walk down main street wondering why every second restaurant is Thai.  I was wandering down a dusty road I obviously didn’t belong on, a truck pulled over to spray me with dirt and gravel and drove off.  I might not look like I belong, but trust me buddy, compared to most people here I’m just a normal guy.

Obviously too quaint and nice and by the lake to even consider parking here

On the other side of the coin, after a night in the mall parking lot (bylaw officer told me to move along after 4 nights at Safeway) I was having my morning coffee and a guy pulled up in his pickup, asked where I was from, then started going off on how it was bullshit they were trying to rustle me along.  Not seeing the NO OVERNIGHT PARKING! bulletin under my wipers yet, I continued to gradually clue is as he told me the place didn’t used to be like this and there weren’t so many damn cops and everybody was free to do as they pleased.  Amen.

It’s still a great place with a lovely beach and main street, I just found because of the geography and culture I was getting claustrophobic, so I left.

Still, kinda hard to complain about
