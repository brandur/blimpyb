Friday’s News: Happy End of the World

Blimpy On December - 10 - 2010

It’s been one of the better news weeks I can remember, and it just keeps getting better.

Miley Cyrus on Salvia

This sounds like a made up YouTube video, and while I wish it was, I’m afraid not.  I haven’t watched it, but reading the story, well, even I have to say this isn’t good.  You remember that article I wrote about smoking DMT?  Yeah, I was joking, I’ve come across some more than once since and somehow managed to refrain.  And while I’ve always sort of hoped Blimpy’s audience is responsible adults, I know Miley’s isn’t.  It makes me shudder thinking about how many teenage girls just became curious to try one of the world’s strongest hallucinogens, and one that I’ve heard more than my fair share of horror stories about.  That shit will mess up your brain, especially if you’re only 18.

Smashing Up Prince Charles’ Car

Burn Baby Burn

This one was newsworthy even before the rioting.  In the UK to help fight the deficit they’ve voted to triple the cap on university tuition from around $5000 to $15000.  How they came up with that as the fix is beyond me, and I think it’s pretty shameful they couldn’t find a way to phase it in more gently, plus removing accessibility to education will likely do far more harm down the road.  So naturally the kids got to rioting in the streets, lighting fires and whatnot, and I don’t blame them.  Somehow Prince Charles and Camilla decided to drive their Rolls Royce near enough to the melee that it got all smashed up and covered in paint, interrupting their lovely evening on their way to a Royal Variety Performance.  This all makes sense, action reaction, but what I don’t get is how CNN the next day has an article about how this is coming up in US too.  Maybe I’m just mad by all the L.A. riots comparisons in the article, but no, it’s straight up sensationalism bullshit, true or not.

Sarah Palin Relating Debt Reduction to…Death Panels?

OK, the whole death panel argument during US healthcare reform that councils of politicians would choose who got to live or die (rather than based on who has the most money right now) was good reactionary partisan fear politics.  To address one of America’s other main concerns, Obama setup the totally bipartisan Simpson-Bowles debt commission (one was a Republican, one a Democrat) and they came up with about 20 fantastic ideas to reduce the national debt.  Naturally the vote to put it before congress didn’t pass with the committee voting roughly along party lines.  But now, Sarah Palin has come out against the recommendations saying that some of their methods for debt reduction are akin to death panels.  Crazy bitch or not, she then goes on to quote her own crazy Republican study about an alternative roadmap that uses a 70% higher GDP forecast than Simpson-Bowles.  Makes it kind of easier to balance the books…

OpenLeaks is Coming

This is the good news of today’s lot, and just what I was saying in my recent WikiLeaks article.  A spinoff is coming where the site won’t be about attention grabbing Julian Assange, just straight up how to get leaks out to the media, plain and simple.  I’m looking forward to even more incarnations.
