Space is the place

Blimpy On January - 19 - 2012

Ah to be back in a Walmart parking lot in the middle of nowhere.  Well, apologies to the residents of Deltona, Florida, but being exactly halfway between Orlando and Daytona Beach, this isn’t exactly a destination.  I did appreciate the naming of the bar across the street as the Sports Den, before even entering there were no bones about the fact I would be surrounded by degenerate 60 year olds bitching about their ex-wives and saying all kinds of shit to the lovely and barely 21 barkeep Sammi knowing she had to put up with it to get her tips.

Open the pod bay doors

FYI, I’ve been doing a belated Christmas with my parents the last 8 days after refusing to return home for the holidays.  It was alright then, but currently is -30 in Calgary, so I stand by my decision to not take the chance.  I’ll take the Kennedy Space Center, Sea World and fresh shrimp over ski hills, overpriced drinks and everyone pretending we don’t live in Siberia any day of the week.

Just to expand on a point I made in my last post (yeah, I know they’re getting less frequent, eat me), I do laugh at the international perception of Americans as jackasses the deeper I get into tourist locations.  I forget how much closer Disney World is than Disney Land to Europe, so the concentration of our friends from across the pond is dramatically higher in Florida than California.  And when you’re standing in a grocery store with a 6 deep line and a 13 year old kid with a thick Italian accent walks right up to the front when the cashier is ringing someone else through and yells “hey, hey, HEY!  How much this costs?!?!” it all becomes clear.  Yes, Americans are loud and obnoxious, and to some degree entitled.  But at that exact moment it made me realize that the type of American that is able to be a globetrotter (only 20% have passports) must have money, probably thinks they’re better than everyone and is an asshole, probably fitting the exact same demographic of Europeans coming here.

R.I.P. Yuri

Ummm, I have no point whatsoever here.  But I took a bunch of fun pictures of Cocoa Beach and Orlando, check them out in the gallery.
