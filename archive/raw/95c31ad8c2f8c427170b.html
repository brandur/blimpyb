British Columbia Politicians Are Crazy Too

Blimpy On July - 28 - 2010

They’re politicians, the fact I expected anything different is shameful.  I guess it’s because there’s this idyllic view of British Columbia being liberal and forward thinking, much like Canada’s California, but unfortunately not always the case.

The issue that triggered my response here is BC government-run online gambling.  It was only two years ago the Canadian federal government banned the use of credit cards for online gambling, which helped curtail its use and got rid of the annoying ads on TV and online.  It also would help curtail the underlying issue, problem gambling, by limiting access to it.  If people have to go to a casino or VLT to get their fix, they’ll be less likely to do it than just sitting down in front of a computer.

Gordon Campbell, BC Premier: Defender of societies weakest members or power-hungry maniac? Why do I even ask.

But no, not happy with their current tax revenues, the BC lottery and gaming commission has opened up an online gambling parlour.  All with the totally reasonable $10,000 limit per day!!  Oh you mean I can only lose $3.65 million dollars in one year?  How kind and generous!

My father taught me from a young age that gambling was a tax on the stupid.  In Vegas they even advertise on the machines 96% payout.  If you put in $100, you’ll wind up with $96.  I guess paying $4 for the rush and chance to win millions is what draws people in, but statistically it’s just not going to happen.

Back to the issue at hand.  So the government uses its online casino as a way to print money so it can…spend it on social programs for problem gamblers?  It’s a classic case of the left hand not knowing what the right hand is doing.  How many people need welfare services after they spend all they’ve got?  Or alcohol abuse, depression and family counselling?  Even worse, say the government sets their rake in the online casino at 10%, well HST is already 12%, so they would have been better off letting the guy spend his $10,000 putting a deposit on a boat so the boat salesman could put a down payment on a car, and so on paying tax all the way down the line.

So even in Canada’s most liberal province, our government that’s supposed to protect the weakest members of society is instead misguidedly preying on them.

Oh, and I found a real reporter who has all the facts and everything, it’s a good read Online Gambling Just Too Profitable To Ignore.  Apparently the excuse is people will gamble anyways offshore, so may as well keep the money in Canada.  I don’t buy it, the government should sell heroin too if that’s how they feel.
