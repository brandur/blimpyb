The Delta Diaries: Blimpy Gets Taught A Lesson

Blimpy On November - 16 - 2011

The only hellhound on my trail towards the grave of Robert Johnson was my own subconscious, no crossroads kneeling or soul selling yet I’m afraid.  I slept as poorly as I have in months the night before which was equal parts apprehension and it being the first day in several not being completely mashed off an aptly brand-named combination of Full Throttle energy drink and Diesel 190 proof grain alcohol.  Fizz Bitch on Day 1, Fizz Bitch on Day 468.

It burns to drink

Through the magic of Couch Surfing I have been living in Indianola MS with 4 girls who work for the Teach For America program, which is equal story fodder to drinking a glass of whiskey with the ghost of the greatest blues musician in history.  Actually, we’re going to have to rewind and come back to Robert Johnson next post (but you can take a look in the gallery).

So, for a variety of historical, socio-economic and political reasons the American education system varies substantially from place to place.  Think Martha’s Vineyard versus the movie Dangerous Minds.  And then there’s of course “the Delta”, arguably the poorest, most unhealthy place in the country.  Although Birmingham Alabama just went bankrupt, so maybe they’ll have some competition.  Anyways, an organization was founded some years ago by a lady named Wendy Kopp to get college graduates not necessarily with education degrees to take two years of their time and be public school teachers at the worst performing institutions in the country, which began in inner city New York.

At least it's beautiful

Obviously part of the reason I’m on my trip across the continent is so the next time I hear someone say anything about somewhere they’ve never been I can snap at them for not having a goddam clue, because I don’t have one either and I lived there for a month.  The Delta is the most complicated place I have been on my entire journey.  That last article I wrote about the personhood amendment and fighting AIDS with more testing, well, the only way the word condom can be legally mentioned in a school classroom in sex ed in this state is in the context of their failure rate.  Naturally the people pushing all these laws are old money rich white folks who would probably just force their kid to get an abortion not to embarrass the family, then not tell anyone.

I went to a museum in Greenwood called Cottonlandia where they had a history wall 30 feet wide full of hundreds of bullet points.  From 1830 to 1910 the word “slave” appeared once, the only mention of the Civil War was a “federal ship” that got stuck in the river near there.

We'll get to Mr. Johnson next time

I hear of a first grade girl who weighs 117 pounds and wets herself in class because she was never potty trained while another boy tries to light the carpet on fire.  Speaking with those lucky enough to teach high school (who immediately agree with me that by then it’s too late to make a significant difference so they have no idea what their purpose is) I hear stories of students threatening to empty a full clip into their teacher’s chest.  Of course this is at a party where I am in the vast minority not being in Teach For America, so everyone is mostly upper-middle class white from the Northeast teaching at black schools in the deep south complaining that it didn’t mesh with their idyllic view of “helping society” while I realize most of them knew less about this area than I did before deciding to move here.

Wafts of “well at least it will look good on a resume” floating through the air further confuse me, but honestly, I’m not knocking.  The Delta Diaries are going to have legs, bear with me.
