Raider Nation

Blimpy On November - 7 - 2010

“The massive Raider Nation is beyond doubt the sleaziest and rudest and most sinister mob of thugs and wackos ever assembled.” – Hunter S. Thompson

Death to the Chiefs

After miles of warehouses and shipping docks, Oakland-Alameda County Coliseum juts up through the fog as we herd off the train.  It is an ominous day and the low-hanging dark clouds have surely been summoned by the black and grey hordes marching to war.  The entrance ramp is a cage lined with barb wire, encasing the menacing, guttural battle cry that would echo for the rest of the afternoon, still into today as I write, perhaps forever.  Raaaaaaaaiderrrrrrrrs.  You are the oldest of the animatronic skeletons on the Pirates of the Caribbean ride that the mechanics could never decommission for fear it would bite their arm off, forged of bone and metal, a black greasy tar of hatred coursing through your veins.

Shuffling around in the nosebleeds to find a decent enough seat where no one will tell you to move, we settle in the second to last row, what your ticket says isn’t relevant at this point.  Within minutes 45 year old Joey in the very last row taps me on the shoulder, flashes his pipe and says “you wanna hit this shit?”  Thinking I am inside an NFL stadium and there is security, I decline.  Two minutes later he insists “hey man, just hit this shit!”  He has already accused me of allegiance to the Chiefs simply because I have red hair, so this offer I cannot decline.  He even gets me to ask the people sitting in front of me.

The Sea of Black and Silver

The stands are very steep, any seat would be good for a view of the sloppiest first half of any game all year.  The rain is coming down hard at times and the Raiders decide to play their 2007 brand of football with a foolhardy fake punt, several fumbles in the mud, dumb penalties and zero points.  Even from afar you can see the winces on the cheerleaders’ faces as they grind their pristine white pleather boots into the sludge, and it’s confusing why they haven’t broken into some form of softcore mud wrestling.

“Fuck you Matt Cassel, I hate you!  You have cock breath Matt Cassel!  Fuck you!”  These were serious yells emanating from all around, not taunting, literally hating the other team’s quarterback.  I had no opinion on the fellow before, I think I despise him now.  One Kansas City fan stupid enough to wear a red poncho with their logo walked the aisle to his seats and three whole sections completely stopped following the game to boo, scream and curse.  Before halftime I’ve already violated the first six items on the Raiders Fan Code Of Conduct, perhaps the greatest who’s kidding who ever put in written word.

The jersey saying STABler is fitting

But the crowd was still more than with it, everyone getting more beers and waiting in the mile-long bathroom line saying “just wait ‘til the second half, we’re coming back”.  First play, kickoff return for a touchdown by Jacoby Ford, then pandemonium.  He would make three other massive catches, finish the day with more than 300 yards overall and he’d only made 4 catches the entire season.  The refs really were trying to screw the Raiders over and despite bad call after bad call amounting to 140 yards in penalties, cue one of the huge plays to Ford with seconds left in the game to get a field goal to tie it and send it to overtime, then the first time the Raiders touched the ball in the extra session the exact same thing and game over.

The sweet smell of victory

On the way out somebody lights up their victory blunt and jokingly asks if anyone has a Kansas City ash tray.  This is a brilliant line I will use the rest of my trip about the local team to indicate my allegiance to the Raider Nation.  A girl wanting to bum a hit tries to play cute with the guy and says “oh hi, my name’s Casey.”  Hearing just what he wanted to hear, the guy spouts back “K.C.?!?!  Fuck you, bitch!”  There is someone wearing a Giants jersey leaning over the railing 5 stories up the exit ramp, thousands are screaming “throw him over!!!”

I hate every football team…no, I hate everything that isn’t black and silver and must drink a can of motor oil at once.
