Kal Tire: Truly Bad Service Since 1953

Blimpy On July - 29 - 2010

Mechanics.  For anyone who’s had to maintain a car, it’s often a sore spot.  Either you don’t know what’s wrong and they take advantage and charge you up the wazoo, or you know exactly what you need done and they do a sloppy job.  Blimpy’s latest tirade falls into the second category, although they still made attempts for it to fall in both.

Bessie is an old RV.  Trudging up mountain passes fully loaded when it’s 30 Celsius outside is very hard on her.  At this point I’m not even sure any more, but it seemed to me like she was overheating as problems got worse the longer I drove.  So I took it into the Kal Tire in Penticton telling them I’m having cooling problems and they say “well, we’ll just do a full mechanical inspection!”  No thanks, I already got one at a Kal Tire in Calgary and you had that on file before you asked, just the cooling system please.  They take a look and find a tiny leak in the radiator, show me the corrosion and it not holding pressure, order me a new one to install in Kelowna at 9:30 Monday, no charge.  Their motto is True Service which I am ridiculing in the title, but at this point I’m a happy customer.

Bessie never leaked a drop before "True Service"

After calling Saturday to again confirm the time, I arrive first thing Monday morning to be told it’ll be just a bit.  In “True Service” speak this means they’ll get around to it at about 12:30.  At least they have free popcorn and coffee and I got to watch Lance Armstrong officially retire 100 times on the sports highlights.

After they finish up around 3 o’clock, I catch the mechanic who worked on it and he says everything went fine, he just had a bit of trouble tightening the gaskets on the hoses.  OK, fair enough, he’s a mechanic, there were difficulties in his job and he overcame them.  I drive it back where I’m staying in Kelowna, crack a beer, watch some baseball and pack up to leave the next day.

About 20 minutes into my drive, I can feel the heat from the engine on my legs and it’s overheating worse than before.  Bessie never leaked a drop before getting “True Service”.  10 more minutes and it’s dire enough I pull over and kill the engine, only to hear a sizzling sound that wasn’t there before and see coolant on the ground before I even pop the hood.  There are 4 gasket hoses to the radiator, there’s green steamy ooze coming out of three of them.

At least the sizzling coolant has a sweet aroma

I pull into the Kal Tire in Merritt, let them know about the poor job from the day before, they say it will be 40 minutes, I go get a sandwich.  This time 40 minutes meant immediately and they say they’re finished when I get back.  The hood’s still up, the mechanic’s still there, I ask “did you tighten all three lose hoses, or just this one?”  “Oh, you wanted all three?”  It then takes him roughly 30 seconds with a gasket tightening tool…why didn’t they just do it right the first time?!?!

In an even funnier coincidence, hours after this when I’d made it to the town of Hope, I got a random feedback call from Kal Tire where I got to rate my overall experience as 3/10.  They even asked if I wanted to leave a message for the Kelowna store manager…no thanks, I’m sure he hears enough swearing already working in the shop.

I think the moral of the story is that I’ve had the best luck just breathing down the neck of mechanics making it seem like I know what’s going on to put the fear of God in them, otherwise they really just don’t care because they already got your money.
