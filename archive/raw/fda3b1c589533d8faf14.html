If a government shutdown closes the National Parks…and I’m in one?

Blimpy On April - 7 - 2011

Do I still exist?  Is Bessie repossessed by Uncle Sam to pay down the debt?  I guess I don’t get $3 back from the weeklong pass I bought?

According to CNN I have 24 hours to leave until they shut the gates.  This seems somewhat ridiculous, if I didn’t have the 3Gs on my phone would I be forced to survive on my reserve stock of pretzels, peanut butter and beef jerky camped at the exit, trapped inside?  I’m almost contemplating sticking around just to find out, especially since I stuck with my temporary fridge cooling solution of filling it with cheap, cold beers even though it works fine now.

Not only is he a jerk, he's an idiot...and he fake cries all the time!!

The real tagline of this post is “how fucking crazy can America get?”  But lest we forget why this is happening, I’ve already mentioned the auspices of debt.  Having a debt ceiling is one of those seemingly good ideas that’s somehow turned around against the American people to hold the economy hostage to squeeze in policy riders.  Republicans want $60 billion in cuts, Democrats say let’s do half, GOP says OK fine, $30 bil, but we have to quit funding public broadcasting and Planned Parenthood and increase military spending.  Dems say…wait, what the fuck?

I wish I was making this shit up.  Yes, increase military spending.  Even Donald Rumsfeld came out saying that the Pentagon gets more money thrown at it than it knows what to do with.  But OK, the military-industrial complex isn’t exactly news…maybe the idea to defund a public health service because some miniscule percentage of all pregnant women who visit it decide of their own free will to get an abortion?  None of this stuff even makes the headlines, especially on Fox which is sadly the most watched news network in the country.

Who needs this stuff anyways?

This isn’t the NFL lockout…or is it?  Why can’t groups of grown men make decisions any time but in the minutes before the deadline while spending all the lead up blowing as hard as possible to the press making the other side look bad instead of trying to make progress?  I make the NFL comparison out of relevancy for myself.  I mean football is a game, an amusement, but really have our elected representatives proven themselves to be anything more than that either?

I pay no taxes, I use no public services other than roads (and National Parks).  Of course I’m not an American either, but have been saying for months now the disconnect between the populace and the politicians that govern it has grown to such insane proportions it almost seems like there must be a tipping point somewhere.  Since I’ve arrived here, tax cuts for the rich have been extended, bank profits and bonuses have reached record levels 2 years after the “collapse”, murals celebrating the labour movement of the last century have been torn from government offices…I could go on forever, and I really just wish I could quit watching, but I can’t because it’s a god damn train wreck.
