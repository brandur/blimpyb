Best. Comment. Ever.

Blimpy On July - 18 - 2012

3 months between posts is no way to run a blog, but I’ve been awoken by a most excellent comment on a post I made about Crescent City CA almost two years ago.  It’s moment like this that make me glad the content I previously posted is just sitting there on the interwebs for all to see and write their take on.

I’ve been living in Memphis for the last 4 months and very soon should update people on the unique structure of this city versus the yuppie paradise I grew up in, post some pictures of the dope garden I am growing or some of the excellent BBQ I’ve been eating.  I’ve held off because it just doesn’t feel the same as when I was trudging across the continent, me and Bessie versus the world.  But regardless, I have reposted the comment from Tracy as well as my response below and for now have miraculously held of on writing more because I believe it all speaks for itself.  Enjoy.

Tracy’s comment:

I have lived in Crescent city and Brookings. And believe me when I say that we are very happy to see narrow minded, communist, Obama loving, vegan burger eating people like you drive right through. Yes it is correct that we have tradional ways and still stand for the US constitution that our old outdated forfathers wrote. If you want to live in a modern more communist like soiciety then move to China or Germany. We like our right to keep and bear arms, our right to freedom of speech. I know your type likes to be told what to do and eat and how to do it by Big Brother. The truth is it’s your type that is killing the good old USA by taking away basic family structure and moral values, by killing us slowly with political correctness, by tieing up all our jobs with inviromental BS. If it wasn’t for you and your type we would still be a manufacturing nation that thrives on it’s own natural resources like wood, oil, farmland and gold ect. Instead we depend on China for almost everything. So really….keep driving!

My reply:

Thanks for writing Tracy! I’m going to dedicate an entire post to your great feedback, I just wanted to clarify a few things first:

-I’m not narrow minded, communist, Obama loving or vegan. One could argue bundling all those terms together in a blanket negative light would make you guilty of the first.

-You mention a modern communist “soiciety” such as Germany, but they actually practice capitalism there.

-I am able to choose my own meals without the help of Big Brother.

-You blame “your type” (i.e. me) for “tieing up all our jobs with inviromental BS” then are upset the US is not thriving due to it’s natural resources such as oil and gold. There is a limited amount of those things in the ground and when they run out we have to a) import them from other countries and b) alter our consumption and manufacturing patterns…it’s not related to the “type” of person you or I represent.

I could go on, but am hoping (likely in vein) to hear more from you soon!
