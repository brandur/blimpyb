Livin’ in a van, down by the Columbia River

Blimpy On October - 3 - 2010

Lord I’ve waited long and hard to drop that reference.  Chris Farley (R.I.P.) folks, the SNL skit is so funny even the other actors can’t keep a straight face.

Best spot ever

But this is actually the first time I’ve camped right next to a river.  Granted it’s a 4 mile wide massive shipping channel, but it was still the absolute bomb and best boondocking site to date.  It will certainly be hard to top.

I couldn’t really think of a great one (suggestions welcome in the comments) but here goes…I might not have a job or a house, but I did stay at a Holiday Inn Express parking lot last night.

I really did stumble on some sort of accidental Astoria, Oregon brilliance here though.  Whatever spot I’m actually parked at, it’s not really clear if it’s Holiday Inn property or the city, although I’m 90% sure it’s the latter.  It’s no man’s land, which is where I reside 24/7.  If a cop drives by, he probably just thinks it’s hotel overflow.  If the hotel actually checked plates versus the guest register, I’m not really on their lot.  And either way, dammit do I have a hell of a view.

Even piles of rusty garbage look sexy here

Astoria is also definitely the coolest small town I’ve passed through in the US.  It’s right on the water, it’s really old for the west coast, it’s big enough to have a nightlife but small enough to still have that quaint town feel.  I wandered for hours seeing all kinds of new and interesting sights, chatted with some real lively and entertaining bums, drank some delicious microbrew, then went on my merry way.  What more could you ask?
