Crescent City: Just Keep Driving

Blimpy On October - 20 - 2010

When the first place you stop in a city to check your map has a guy in a doo rag with a greasy moustache start slink-walking across the street towards you asking “hey esse, can I…’help’…you with anything?” do what I did, drive.  Drug dealers don’t use the finger quote sign for the quote/unquote I indicate around ‘help’, they say “hey esse, can I…” then look suspiciously both directions while tilting their head sideways and giving the one finger moustache wipe.

Great. Just great.

When the second place you stop in a city is the Tourist Information Center and it’s only open 10 to 4, that probably means they don’t get many tourists.  And when you walk up to the door to discover this and right next to the hours is a $1000 reward for information on who is using kittens as bait in crab traps, do what I did, drive.  I mean Jesus Christ, yeah that’s heinous, but to advertize it to people who’ve never been to your town before?  You think whoever hangs out with cat snatchers spends their time trawling for brochures?

At least I got some rock shots

Working my way out of their dilapidated downtown back towards the safety of the Walmart parking lot before sundown, I almost felt like rolling up my window.  There are boarded up stores, more vagrants than anywhere I’ve been yet and just generally terrible vibes.  At least I’ve wizened up from Westport, and this place is way worse, I can’t even imagine it after dark.  Which really sucks for Crescent City, it’s a night and day difference from the bright and bustling Brookings just 15 miles north on the other side of the Oregon-California border.

I guess people prefer Walmart discount beer

Even going into the Walmart for supplies is a trip down depressing lane.  All the customers are as run-down as the town and generally real fat, which is no surprise looking at their selections.  They don’t even sell skim milk.  You want a whole-wheat bagel?  Tough, they got plain, cheese and onion.  Three of the ten food aisles are dedicated to Halloween candy.  Looking at mountains of 36-cases of beer, I asked if they had anything smaller, of course not.  Yeah it’s only $20, great deal, but who the hell needs 36 beers?  You can’t buy that many in one package anywhere in Canada, people shouldn’t be encouraged to do so, especially in a broken down shit town!

Heebie-jeebie central

Wanting to at least give the place another chance the next morning and get some pictures, I rolled through once more.  Half the town was destroyed by a tsunami in 1964, so you gotta have a little sympathy.  But that was over 40 years ago, things seem stuck at that point in time, and while the thick, ominous fog makes for a good shot, it doesn’t help the vibe.  There was even a microbrewery on my map I wanted to check out.  Well, before I got to town that is, if I left Bessie alone for an hour I doubt there’d be much left of her.  And sure enough the brewery was abandoned and boarded up, right next to the abandoned gas station, both right on the carless, empty Front Street.  You get the idea.

Why did I even come to Crescent City again?  Oh right, to keep on driving.
