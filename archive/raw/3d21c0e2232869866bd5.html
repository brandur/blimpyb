The Grass Is Always Greener

Blimpy On August - 19 - 2011

Thou shalt not covet has always been funny to me, there’s nothing more human.

“I was born and raised in Calgary, but I live in a van in Amarillo Texas.”  There was a pause.  We glanced at each other sideways.

Writing bullshit on a computer screen.  Crawling along the edge of a straight razor…and surviving.  Time…space…I think I need counselling to stop watching the lady on French Food At Home and singing the theme song to myself when I’m not.

It’s hard to get inspired when you’re not out colliding yourself with the world, but that will recommence in exactly one month.  In the meantime, here’s some macro photos I took in Calgary.
