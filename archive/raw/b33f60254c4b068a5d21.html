Mudshark Brewery

Blimpy On March - 8 - 2011

After hearing that there’s really nothing to do in Lake Havasu City except drink at karaoke or look at the actual original London Bridge they shipped in from London, I decided one afternoon was enough so I may as well stop at the brewery on the way through as I had originally planned. To hear the bartender tell the early 20’s girl there with her father that she needed to get out of town and finish school because this was the worst place on earth for someone her age confirmed my feelings from walking around town earlier.

The front facade

Oh, and in terms of credibility, this was no ordinary bartender, he knew what was up. Having free reign to create his own drink of the day, this Friday’s concoction was dubbed the Barley Sheen and consisted of a Blue Moon pint with a shot of vodka and a sugar lined rim. “You can guess what the sugar stands for” he said with a wry smile.

I started off with a seasonal cream ale, which had too much going on at first, it was light and fluffy, yet still with a bit of musk. Not a negative musk mind you, and it mellowed into a very slight bitterness which let me notice the beer was also a touch sweet halfway through. Still, too much going on, sort of like a Budweiser on major steroids, but all in all not a negative experience.

Too lazy to swap out my wide angle lens

Being $3 pint and ½ price appy happy hour, I tagged on an order of buffalo chicken fingers and their Scorpion Amber Ale, which I had seen recommended highly online, and rightfully so, I should have skipped straight to it. It was malty and sweet but had a bit of hops to temper it, and strangely enough for any of my Calgary readers, happened to remind me exactly of a Trad on a good day. Buffalo chicken fingers are sort of impossible to screw up, but these ones had a good sauce and not only were massive but seemed like they were meant to be washed down with the Scorpion Amber Ale. It’s probably also a good thing as by this point I had made three unusually witty remarks by my normally low standards that had caused the 20-something girl to keep staring at me out the corner of her eye and initiate conversation to the point that her dad leaned forward enough to put an outright stop to it, because I was covered in Frank’s Red Hot sauce.

Taste: 3/5

Value: 3.75/5
