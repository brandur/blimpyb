Portland: Drink, smoke, mope

Blimpy On October - 11 - 2010

“A microbrewery?  Yeah, they just opened one up around the corner.”

Waiting until just after the dealer has left to ask and get the obvious answer, it’s been a good first 30 minutes couch surfing in Portland.  She was jittery as hell, sipping a plastic coffee thermos full of wine she brought with her as she drove up (at 3:30 in the afternoon).  A clean-cut, skinny, good-looking girl my age who reveals she also works a paper route at 1 AM every day, has the perfect Martha recipe (referred to only in first name) to make cookies with if she could find some cheap shake, if you have a Portland public library card you can download their entire audio book collection for free and that it’s bullshit her credit score got hurt just because she had one measly car repossessed.

Funny drawing on the fridge

Three pipes, two bongs, one vaporizer lay scattered about the living room of the two girls I’m crashing at.  The dealer packs two bowls for everyone.  It would be bowls the entire stay in town, in the alley behind the bar or on the front porch, all buzz, no session.  Pass it quick with your hand cupped over the embers if it’s cherried.  Res hits later when there’s nothing left.  I ask what there is to do here, and who’s famous that isn’t a musician.  Drinking and nobody.

Sam, the guy with the big afro and cop shades has been chillin’ the whole time, but hops on his bike to get to Eugene.  A seemingly cheery fellow, on his return Monday he pronounces he will be killing himself Wednesday.  That first evening though, he’s replaced by two guys who explain Cascadian separatism to me complete with talk of tactics and munitions and shipping routes and strategic mountain passes drawn on cocktail napkins.  One of them goes on to get the Cascadia flag tattooed on his forearm as a one hour break in the middle of the Friday bar night, a self-reward for being laid off from his architecture job that afternoon.  “Sally, that tattoo your grandpa has is from when he wanted to secede from the Union.”

Captures the dinge pretty well

The other fellow it turns out gets squeamish at talk of blood, so someone’s random explanation of a trip to the donor clinic has him walk to the other side of the bar, pass out, get picked up by the strangers around him, throw up all over himself and leave.  Maybe he read Catch 22 too many times, I dunno, I’m getting another $2 PBR and heading back to our table to watch more karaoke as the place picks up.  Portland’s Best Refreshment they tell me.

Can't help it, sorry

By the end of the night I’m back at the same place, splitting a pizza and more bowls, just one of the now 5 gals.  I don’t say more than what’s necessary, but tales of a knitted cap being called a toque, that it’s “Canadian” Thanksgiving this weekend, ardently agreeing with their railing against anti-choice GOP gubernatorial candidate Chris Dudley and being able to blow smoke rings seems to work.  At least enough to merit the random graffiti they have here on stop signs “Canada STOP Being Cute”.  Hey, we can’t help it.

The next morning I try and steal some WiFi, two of the networks are named ether and whippets.  Their mayor is named Sam Adams.  Two British girls arrive at the end of the weekend to couch surf as well, I ask them why they have come to travel America, they say a fortune teller told them it was their destiny.  We spend the evening with a bartender/garage band musician fellow going for a Ray Liotta look who loooovvvessss everything and insists we listen to his record collection that’s way heavy on the Dylan, Velvet Underground and T-Rex.  I’ll let him have his fun, may as well just fit in with everybody else and drink, smoke and mope.
