Blimpy needs your help!

Blimpy On June - 10 - 2011

I’m trying to get voted in as the Calgary Stampede Ultimate Social Media Intern and it would be a huge boost if people could vote for me every day from now until June 17th and repost that link wherever possible.  See the newest Blimpy video and vote for me here please!!  (Oh, and as usual it’s cooler in HD, this time only 720p…and if you want to read what the contest is for check here)

Not only will you be helping me get backstage access and my own trailer to a 10 day, 24 hour carnival of awesomeness, you’ll be helping to ensure all kinds of ridiculous content gets generated come July!

Quite the difference

The video was crafted in exactly 24 hours with my friend filming and me starring and editing.  I’m kind of a fan of the 60 second idea because it forces me to not ramble.  Also, we used a new Technicolor picture style on my camera where it films everything super flat and I have to add all the saturation, contrast and sharpness back in post, but as you can see from the before and after screenshot, I think it’s worth it and will likely be using this style for all future videos.

Also, I apologize in advance, but expect to see a lot of Facebook and Twitter updates this week reminding you to vote every day.  All we have to do is make the top 10 and the judges hand-pick a winner from there, and I legitamitly like our chances!

I hope you’re looking forward to a summer of awesomeness as much as I am!
