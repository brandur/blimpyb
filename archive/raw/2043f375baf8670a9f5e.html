North America is my bitch

Blimpy On January - 9 - 2012

The Atlantic.  Wait, the Atlantic Ocean?  The Atlantic anticlimax?  As quickly as the Georgia pines of last week turned to palm trees, so too did the New Mexico desert of 8 months ago give way to sandy Florida beaches and me staring towards Spain.

Now this is happenning, the Atlantic

So FYI, it’s not that difficult to drive all the way from coast to coast in America, even if it takes you 12 months.  And you’ll see a lot, and eat and drink just as much.  You might also find that camping under the stars in the middle of nowhere is just as rewarding as plopping down on a bar stool next to one of the 300 million people that live here.  They’re all very different depending on where you go, but football and beer some to be pretty common threads.

I lost track of where I was going anymore sometime around the middle of Texas.  Now I’m just a guy in a van.  A friend wondered this before I even began my journey, when I would become “guy in van”, and that happened at the exact same time in middle Texas.  In the beginning it was like “wheee, look at me go!”, but then it gave way to “where am I going?”  But the real distinguishing mark is I don’t remember what it was like to think this whole idea was new and fresh and explain it to people like some revelation, because it’s not, it just is.  Not that I wouldn’t highly recommend it, and Bessie will be for sale in maybe 2 years when I get sick of this, but for now the south is pretty fantastic.  I also highly recommend quitting your job and telling anyone who will listen to play a game of hide and go fuck themselves.

Ocean still got forests

It’s become painfully obvious that anyone who doesn’t stop and say hello crossing paths with you on a nature trail or a not overly crowded city street is most likely from Europe, and the fact they generally start speaking French, German or Italian immediately thereafter is telling.  But even if they spoke fluent English, they wouldn’t stop to say hello, and I don’t know why.  You’d think I would have some insight, having never done it myself back in Canada, but it’s something American I can’t explain, same as how I picked up saying “riiiiight” every time I agree with someone.

The Florida coast is somewhat similar to the California coast, but with less money, which equates to more charm if you aren’t rolling around with a few thousand in your pocket.  Jacksonville Beach, St. Augustine, sure there’s plenty of nice stuff, but you don’t have to be a millionaire to live there, and a bar with a $2 pint or a pornography super-warehouse are never too far.  Even Jekyll Island in Georgia where I took all these shots, I didn’t even clue in as to why there were 4 golf courses on a tiny island with a campground, then getting there to find a castle, marina and “Millionaire’s row”, well I was still able to camp in a lovely spot of $20 a night, and I’ll gladly spend that all the way down to the everglades.
