The Giants Win the World Series!

Blimpy On November - 2 - 2010

The only consequence of my first shot at real time journalism is my hand and shoulder hurt a lot from high fiving.  But I did get a free beer from a random guy on the street, watched several people with flags hanging out sunroofs honking like mad and even got some peripheral champagne spray.

Take me out to the ball game

The Giants haven’t won the World Series since they moved to San Francisco in 1958.  There weren’t any major league baseball teams west of the Mississippi when they last won as a franchise in 1954.  Unemployment was 2.9%, gas cost 17 cents a gallon, the polio vaccine and transistor radio had just been invented.  They interviewed a woman who was three years old in 1958, heard news of the team coming to town and was terrified, calling “mommy mommy, giants are coming!!”

Thing is, they really weren’t supposed to win this year.  They made the playoffs on the last game of the season.  Their best batters statistically were grizzled mediocre veteran Aubrey Huff and 23 year old pure rookie Buster Posey, the darling of the entire city.  He’s apparently already married, I’d like to hear the Vegas odds on that lasting.

McCovey Cove is damn cool

But in the old baseball adage the big money Yankees and Red Sox have helped wash from purists’ memories, the Giants had pitching and defence.  The best in the league as a matter of fact.  You only need three hits if you get them in a row and the third one’s a home run, just ask Edgar Renteria.  He also won the World Series with a swing of the bat in 1997, I’d like to hear the Vegas odds on him retiring on top of the goddam world.

And their opponents, the Texas Rangers, were the best hitting team in the league.  It’s an ideal matchup The Onion did a great spoof of, but also turned into a biting culture clash side battle, Dallas VS San Fran.  After the first game there was a clip that’s now legendary here of Dallas reporter Newy Scruggs stopping during his telecast saying surprised “right over there, there’s people smoking weed!”

To the victor go the spoils

Another great dig from Steve Blow of the Dallas Morning News “I’m surprised they still play baseball in San Francisco, I figured by now someone here would have decided that baseball chalk isn’t Earth-kind or that the game is overtly sexist or gender-role confining.”  Damn that’s a good burn, but your team still lost buddy, Texas is a football state is all they need to rebut with here, amongst the obvious other thousands of reasons to make fun of Texas they don’t even bother with.

So now City Hall is lit up orange, there’s people climbing on busses and lighting bonfires, calling it the best day of their lives and rolling magic marker-sized joints to match.  Who am I to declare hockey fans are crazier, this is amazing!  See you at the victory parade Wednesday!
