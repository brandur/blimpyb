Arcata: Front Row Seats with the Freaks

Blimpy On October - 23 - 2010

“Have you ever spoken to a person not from this PLANET?!” a haggard, white-bearded 60 year old asked me and my new bar buddy outside the Sidelines Sports Bar in Arcata’s main plaza.  I replied that if I had I didn’t notice at the time, so the fellow continued “then how do you know anything, maaan?”

Even the cop was a freak, but I didn't put a camera in his face

“Oh I admit the human mind is limited, is that what you’re getting at?” I asked, wanting to see where this went.  He informed me no, that most certainly was not his point, then went right into some rant about aliens, complete with the whole lights in the forest at night and everything.  I slinked away quietly as my new bar buddy took the reins and lambasted the fellow before heading back inside.

You see, my new bar buddy was an attorney.  Wanting to watch my first Calgary Flames hockey game of the season, I discovered online there was a hockey bar in the most hippie city in northern California, but as fate would have it during the first intermission a fellow sat down next to me, asked if he could buy me a beer in exchange for talking my ear off, and I gladly obliged.  He more than kept his end of the bargain over the next three hours, and I certainly learned a thing or two from the complete stranger more than twice my age.

Freaky rhubarb

As the evening was winding down and we had become pretty decent chums, he offered to e-mail another lawyer buddy of his in San Francisco who has season’s tickets to the Sharks NHL team.  He was apparently owed some variety of favour and called it in the form of me getting seats to a game, which I am told are front row.  His friend replied within 20 minutes saying he would “hook me up” with whatever game I wanted to see, and seeing as my hometown Flames will be in San Jose on Nov. 13, I asked if they were available.

How these things happen I do not know, but I was wearing the fedora, so that explains most of it.  Someone else told me it must be karma, and I did only get charged for 1 beer at a casino and informed them I’d actually had 2 just a couple days prior, but this still seems a disproportionate rationing of fortune by the cosmos.  Naturally the San Fran lawyer wrote back that he split the seats so didn’t have the Flames game, but would be happy to sell me ones against Anaheim for $85 a pop…perhaps this is why they’re called shysters.

Freaky bus

Back to the alien-loving goof from the start of the story, this is definitely his town, not mine or the lawyer’s.  You remember at the end of Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas where Johnny Depp is driving back to California and says “just another one of the freaks”?  Yeah, that’s Arcata.  I even mentioned that to a local and he liked the analogy and told me there were a lot of late 1970’s, trip’s supposed to be coming down types who came to Arcata so that it would never stop, and the young people coming through have picked up a similar vibe.

At first I was pretty worried wandering around town and having the first person I actually talked to be another 60 year old telling me to stick around until Halloween to go to the orgy on top of the warehouse he owned.  I also noticed a whole ton of grimeys wandering about, who I at first thought were straight up transients, but no, it’s right at the very peak of clipping season, they’re just a bunch of kids like me (but without the RV) trying to make a buck.

Freaky graffiti

But along with that, and the same townie VS hippie tension I’ve noticed in plenty of spots, this is the first place in America where random street greetings were not cordial and eye contact was slanted and probing.  I noticed it in Seattle and all the way down since, most Americans on the West Coast have it absolutely down pat to do a smile and a nod, or a “hey, how’s it going” without really stopping for the answer.  But that doesn’t matter, it’s still a very welcoming approach that I noticed was completely abandoned in Arcata for a much more glaring sense of caution.  Perhaps out of fear of being dragged into a conversation about aliens and healing crystals.
