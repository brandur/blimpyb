Lessons from the Okanagan: Whatever DMT is, I need to smoke some

Blimpy On July - 25 - 2010

“Oh man, you gotta try DMT!”

Nice weather around these parts

This is the start of terrible or excellent advice, no in betweens.  I actually began the conversation by asking “what’s that drug that makes everything turn into a slinky made out of cubes?” having read about it years ago on a site everyone should know about www.erowid.org.  I wouldn’t have known that eating 4 heaping tablespoons of nutmeg gets you sort of woozy and makes you dumb as a brick for two days unless I’d tried it myself…

“DMT!  I haven’t had my mind blown like that in years!”  This is already leading me to believe we’re in the terrible advice category.  Having promised myself at the trip’s outset “no heavy psychedelics”, I’m pretty much already booked to have pupils the size of dinner plates on mushrooms a week from now.  It’s half-terrifying, but equally exhilarating, we can save most of that for another post, I’ll just dabble here.  See my problem with mushrooms is you can’t turn them off, everything just gets more and more intense until your brain explodes and you realize time and space don’t exist; it takes a couple days to put the pieces back together.  When you aren’t a functional member of society though, not a big deal.

Set the controls for the heart of the sun

Even Wikipedia goes so far as to describe DMT as “the businessman’s trip” because it only lasts 15 minutes and the user is still in control and lucid, which is how it was also described to me from first-hand experiences.  It also states “Near-death and mystical states were relatively rare”.  It was even first synthesized by a Canadian!  Now I have to try it, as harmless as basketball and penicillin!

So yeah, just a heads up there’ll be a follow-up on whether the rumours are true sometime in the not too distant future.
