Six Rivers Brewery – Manager’s Special

Blimpy On October - 22 - 2010

I guess it’s not surprising, but it’s way easier to write bad reviews than good ones.  So in case you didn’t get your fill…

Not hot

When the very friendly manager and owner lady came to my table to introduce herself and ask how things were, I didn’t want to just shit all over her establishment, so I told her the straight up truth “I’m just on my way out actually”.  With her pressing further I decided to gently continue “well, your Death Wings weren’t hot at all” which she basically just shrugged off and said I should have asked for ‘extra’ hot.  Ummm…lady, you sell 6 kinds of wings on the menu and this one was rated 10 out of 10 and I’m not from here.

She somehow parlayed this into a story about some guy who comes in all the time and orders them and yadda yadda…then she’s telling me “well you know this is the second female owned brewery in California”.  OK, lots of Greek people own pizzerias, all I care about is the pizza.  She then asked me if I liked the gorgeous view, having sat in front of the largest window in the restaurant.  “What view?” I asked, to which she replied “oh, well you kind of have to stand up and look into the distance and if there weren’t all these trucks parked here…ohh, so the wings weren’t hot enough?  How about I give you a free taster of our Chili Ale!”

The "view"

I explained to her that I had a Chipotle Ale at Rogue that was painful to drink, so she told me then I certainly must try theirs as it was their brewmaster’s specialty and he was one of the best in the country.  Laughing to myself at that last statement as she went to the bar to get the taster, when she returned I took a sniff first and it was downright spicy.  Taking a sip was just the same, capsaicin mixed with lager basically, sort of a thinned out prairie fire.

“Thaat’s….interesting….” I told her, not wanting to cause a fuss.  She then listed off a number of prizes I’d never heard of that the Chili Ale had won, most of them second or third place, probably for most insane idea that was still barely potable.

Selection and pricing was decent

Somewhere near this point in time I did have an epiphany, although one I will never execute.  What if I just said “I’m a freelance beer and food critic and this place is getting a really bad score on both.”  I don’t really care enough to get free stuff or have her suck my dick some more because I think she may have gathered what I was up to, but I’ve already had three establishments I thought positively of thank me after reading the reviews, so it’d be interesting to see what happens on the flip side.  It’s not ethical though, you’re not supposed to do that, so I don’t, and won’t.  I just stir the pot in words, I didn’t like her place but she doesn’t know that and she left happy, I don’t want to offend her straight to her face after she was kind enough to give me a free sample.

Regardless, not digging the very loud atmosphere (or messed up beers), I headed down the street to park Bessie at a large K-Mart parking lot, wanted to watch the end of the baseball game and wound up at the many fold better Carmela’s Mexican Restaurant.  They let me sit at the bar, on my own stool, chat with the people next to me and didn’t even care if I ordered a beer.  I did just because of this and at my neighbour’s recommendation wound up with an Anderson Valley Boont Amber, which was really delicious and way better than anything at the Six Rivers Brewery.
