Day 1: Igor. Nice guy, not much of a mechanic.

Blimpy On July - 3 - 2010

After working until 4 PM wiring up the solar panels and packing the last of my worldly possessions into Bessie, it was finally time to get the show on the road.  The plan, spend July long at Kokanee Springs near Nelson with a halfway overnight stop in Moyie.  The execution, well, we made it to Cranbrook which is only 20 clicks short, except the last 40 were via tow truck.

Some final touches...like mounting the solar panels and having a door that locks

When you buy an RV the same age as yourself, certain quirks are to be expected.  Flat tire, dead battery, leaky roof, so be it.  Some are even kinda funny, like the headlights making the right turn signal not work.  Now you’d think a computer engineer like myself would see this as a potential problem, pushing button A also happens to push B and C.  But Bessie still drives fine, so I’ll chalk it up under “cute”.

Now, when the sun sets and turning on the headlights going 80 km/h also disables the cruise control, big deal, it pushes button D too, don’t use cruise control or turn right at night.  This is all well and good, until you see the sign “Litter Barrel Ahead” (which is akin to a roadside turnout) and you have to take a piss.  After getting going again, we make it to 20 km/h, Bessie tries to shift to 2nd, the pedal stops working, we stall and pull over on the shoulder at the end of a passing lane as dusk turns to darkness.  To all the truck drivers who roared by, I totally meant to stall there with half the lights not working just to fuck you over and will be sure not to next time after the chorus of air horns.  Oh, and I’m going to start littering and pissing my pants while I drive too.

Still, other than the off chance of witnessing a 10 ton slaughter smash of my months of hard work, I have an AMA membership and a tow truck is on the way.  Igor the Russian rolls up blaring Paul Oakenfold and takes us a mile up the road so he can disconnect the drive shaft.  Bessie is rear-wheel drive and getting towed from the front, we don’t want to mess up the transmission.

Bessie's first boondocking site at Big Blue

Along the way we hear great stories of Igor’s time living in Tel Aviv and Toronto, he makes a good quip about Blimpy Backgammon being Tyler Durden and how I never sleep, drops us off at Big Blue Auto, says he’s tired and goes home.

Waaaiiit a second…drive shaft.  The electrical problems I can fix, but that?  I made myself a list of questions to ask the mechanic, the last being “What does brain do?” (in reference to the electronics box) and realized it was time for bed to try and catch them when they opened…only to hear the title of this post being the first thing the guy at Big Blue had to say…
