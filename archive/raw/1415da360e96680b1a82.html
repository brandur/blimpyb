The RV Graveyard

Blimpy On July - 23 - 2010

Radiators, hub caps, mufflers, transmissions, all of these once belonged to Bessie’s fallen comrades.

Little scrap heap

Out back of an RV shop can be a terrifying place.  This scrap metal once functioned, it had purpose.  But now?

Perhaps it is because I know what is to become of it, having been there 5 years ago.  Steel is the most recycled material in the world, up to 97% of new steel is made with old steel.  It all dawned on me suddenly, gripping a walking bridge guard rail that it was made from Model T’s and confiscated handguns just like the ones I watched the cops throw in the furnace days earlier.

Big scrap heap

Or instead the riser pipes on BP’s Horizon well.  Offshore grade stainless steel has very high Molybdenum and Nickel content and gives off a thick black polluting smoke that often doesn’t conform to environmental codes.  So how do they still make it?  At night so nobody can see.  It’s worth a lot of money.

The steel terminology for this is "dropping a charge in the heat"

Bessie’s steel is not stainless, she rusts and degrades just like a person.  Me, I got a bad shoulder.  Bessie, she got a leaky radiator.  But it can be replaced with a new one made out of melted down old radiators.  It’s a beautiful, terrifying thing.  20 years from now, your shiny new refrigerator will be 0.01% Bessie, having travelled to the redwood forests, Mobile Alabama and the Florida Keys.  Some say each and every one of us has carbon atoms from Abraham Lincoln or Jesus.

OK, enough screwing around, I just wanted to write myself up an excuse to post steel mill pictures.  But to finish off in the same failed philosophical vein I started in…without me, Bessie is nothing.  But without Bessie, I’m still Blimpy.
