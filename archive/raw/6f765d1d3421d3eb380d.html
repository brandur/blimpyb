The Mehserle Sentence Makes Me Sick

Blimpy On November - 5 - 2010

For those of you not from the Bay Area, the Johannes Mehserle case is big news right now, reaching its unjust conclusion during his sentencing today.  On New Years Day 2010 after a night out on the town, Oscar Grant and some cohorts riding the BART (Bay Area Rapid Transit) were called in for making a disturbance and apprehended by the police.  After he was laying face down on the ground with an officer standing on top of him holding his hands to cuff him, Mehserle shot Grant in the back and killed him.

I wish I could shoot someone and only get 7 months

I could go into all kinds of pretexts and side issues, but that last sentence is all you really need to know.  Sure you could say he was resisting arrest, but the cops had him subdued.  Yeah, Grant had prior drug convictions, but that’s really not relevant.  Mehserle’s defence was that he thought his gun was a taser…ummmm.  He got sentenced to 4 years, but including time served, some sort of 2-for-1 I don’t understand and with good behaviour he could be released in 7 months.  7 months for shooting an unarmed man being held on the ground and ending his life.

Stranger still is other BART passengers videotaped the whole thing on their cell phone cameras, and it was some sort of “landmark” that this was admissible in court, yet it still didn’t help Oscar Grant get justice.  Not even the part where Mehserle’s fellow officer Tony Pirone is reported to have punched Grant in the face a few times and called him racial epithets.   Even on the news tonight they called it a landmark that Mehserle got any time at all because it’s “hard” to prosecute cops?  Sooo…anything hard isn’t worth doing?

Even worse was the news teams covering live in Oakland just waiting, even hoping as you could construe from their tone, that there would be further riots.  When Mehserle was convicted only of involuntary manslaughter in July, having the murder and voluntary counts dismissed (after the case was moved to LA to be tried and there were no black jurors), there actually were riots in Oakland with dozens arrested and thousands of dollars in property damage.

And they even got their wish, you can read about it here, along with more background on the case.  I’m sure the cops were chomping at the bit too saying “Yay!  We get to break out the riot gear and assault vehicles and bust some heads again!”  Uhhh, guys, how do you think you got in this mess in the first place?

There is no justice.  Well, unless you’re a cop and get tried by a different set of standards than everyone else.
