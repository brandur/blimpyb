2010: The West Coast in 50 Pictures

Blimpy On January - 2 - 2011

I may have called the gallery the Best of 2010, and for the most part it is, but this is basically the summarized photojournal version of how I got from Canada to Southern California over the last six months.  It also served as an excellent Christmas card I could email to friends and family!

Cropped & Photoshopped

Worth noting is that I took every single one of these shots with my Sigma 30 mm prime lens, being able to zoom is overrated when you’re taking generic walkaround shots in comparison to getting better sharpness and contrast from a nicer lens, especially because you can just crop them to look bigger after the fact.  As the lady at the store was telling me when I picked it up, a fixed focal length forces you to think more about framing your shots and where to position yourself.  I only seem to have really started taking heed of this about halfway through the journey, after San Francisco there’s a marked improvement in the photos, well, at least in my head.  This also corresponds with when I started really Photoshopping the RAW images to add more pop.

For those following from the beginning, you’ll have seen all the shots before, but I did go back and touch up a lot of the earlier ones.  A good example is the freaky guy wading around in the public fountain in Seattle, I was able to get rid of a lot of the glare now that I have a slight clue what the hell I’m doing 4000 pictures in.  And believe me, 2011 is going to have a lot more than 8000 shots.

This post is just meant to be about the pictures, the journey and the year in review so I’ll spare you the camera mumbo jumbo, but once my new wide angle lens and macro extension tubes show up in the mail I’ll definitely have a lot more to say.  But for now I’ll shut up and let the gallery do the talking.

I must be in a good mood or something because I don’t think I’ve said this in a single word on the site yet, but thanks for following along on the ride!  Plenty more madness to come….oh, and I might actually start taking pictures of people next year too!
