But this one goes to 11

Blimpy On May - 10 - 2010

All the electronic & speaker gadgets going into Bessie…

Lots of gadgets, lots of them dirt cheap from China

Essential reading material

My Tymphany LAT-500 transducer subwoofer. It's a lot bigger than it looks in the picture.

My Tang Band W6-1721. It weighs about 15 lbs, that magnet ain't no joke.

The complete set. Trying to decide which of the crappy speakers go in the front door panels

Beautiful, beautiful sound. Can't wait to install it and fire it up...yeehaw.

My Car PC. It has no hard drive. Running XP off a USB key is a bitch and a half, but worth it...once I figure out how to make a backup.

Touchscreen, again from China, again dirt cheap. Slightly frightened to tear the casing off the LCD on the laptop, but oh well.

Not my picture, just hypothesizing about using open baffle speakers instead of cabinets and mounting them in the bed cab up top. Hell of a big and fun canvas to paint something on though.
