I should start selling healing crystals

Blimpy On April - 24 - 2011

“Which way to the energy vortex?”

Yeah! Cuz crystals and aura photography totally exist!

The fact an elderly couple would approach me at a trailhead in Sedona (gallery here) to ask such a question is somewhat depressing if it means I look like I would know the answer.  Although apparently 64% of all visitors to the valley (same number as visit the Grand Canyon) say they come in search of a “spiritual experience”.  I would rate any day that I drove Bessie more than 20 feet and survived a “spiritual experience”, if not every single day over the course of the last ten months, so I guess it depends who you ask.

The story goes that in the early 1980’s, a local woman named Page Bryant came up with the idea while at the hairdressers or under the influence of several gin and tonics, the story differs here and there.  Sorry, when I say “came up with” I mean “had a vision”.  A local clairvoyant clocking in around 400 pounds, she had the brilliant vision that 4 natural energy vortexes could be found in the valley just outside the parking lots of 4 of the already pre-existing highly popular tourist hikes in the area.  And naturally the best way to share this spiritual wealth with the rest of the world would be to sell guidebooks and hire out spiritual tour guides and partner with the largest Jeep tour company in town.

Oh, except for the one she put on the mesa where the airport is.  No matter how clearly marked her maps were, tourists kept wandering onto the tarmac when planes were taking off and landing which made the operator very mad at her, until she had another vision that the vortex had moved near a parking lot slightly down the hill and out of the way.

Spiritual, yes. Bought at a store, no.

Don’t get me wrong though, the red rock cliffs and giant mesas are breathtaking to say the least, and I’m glad I got a chance to see them and hike around, it just kind of drives me watching the nouveau riche either be taken in or attempt to profit from New Age medicine, or religion or whatever bullshit you want to call it when it clearly doesn’t exist.  Of course I did the analysis to see if creating a website selling crap about energy vortexes would be worth the money, and luckily the answer is no.

Still, check the gallery.  And the upcoming 1000 year old Montezuma Castle built into a canyon wall then largest meteor crater in North America are sure to impress.  But not as much as Blimpy the video.  It’s coming Tuesday (once I get close enough to a non-cell phone internet connect).  Look out.
