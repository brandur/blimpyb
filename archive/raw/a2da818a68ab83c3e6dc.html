Mo’ Macro, Mo’ Problems

Blimpy On February - 10 - 2011

I guess I’m doing well if these are my only problems.  And they make for a hell of a gallery.

Problem 1: Everything is a macro shot

Put a macro lens on your camera (or in my case the kit lens with a Canon Extension Tube as explained previously) and you’ll want to take pictures of anything you can find.  Bottle caps, cigarette butts, rotting fruit, it’s all fair game.  I still haven’t gotten past the “woah, that’s cool” phase, but hopefully the charm will wear off soon enough so pedestrians aren’t wondering what I’m doing taking pictures of dirt while sitting in a flower bush.

Problem 2: Lens fever

A common SLR photographer problem, I spent four hours yesterday looking at lenses I could never afford wanting several of them.  Yeah, my Canon Extension Tube job for $80 works pretty damn well, but if I bought a $600 macro lens think how sharp they’d be!  And I wouldn’t need to be 2 inches away from the subject.  Then if I got one that was long enough to do telephoto too I could get a tele-extender and…d’oh!  Livin’ in a van guy budget.

Problem 3: The sticky green is too awesome

In what I’m pretty sure is my first pre-planned shot of the entire trip, a friend suggested I try my macro tricks on the sticky icky.  While definitely not the greatest shot, if I could get the right lighting and a tripod rigged up, High Times Magazine here we come.  They’re nifty looking lil’ buggers up close, eh?  And delicious too.  So, so delicious.

Problem 4: I should be doing real work

This is an affliction that has just started hitting me during the last week.  I could wander around shooting photos, drinking beer and indulging problem 3 all the freaking time, then two years from now I’d be completely screwed in life.  I can actually do a solid 6 hours of work now without burning out, and while there’s no need to ramp it all the way up to a crazy 8 hours, I do feel the need to go park somewhere in the middle of the Arizona desert (perhaps Quartzite) and start hammering away at some other projects.
