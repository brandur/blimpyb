Deja-vu All Over Again

Blimpy On August - 5 - 2011

It’s hard to describe the sheer elation I feel being in the exact same spot in Vancouver I was a year ago.  Then I was just coming to grips with my newfound powers, ranting about self-actualization, feeling out the true boundaries of being free as a bird.  4000 miles and 365 days later, I still feel somewhat the same, except now more seasoned and in control of exactly how far I can go.  The answer…far.

Vancouver's a hell of a town

Nothing has changed on Broadway Street in Kitsilano save a new dollar store or two opening.  I walk by a Mexican restaurant two blocks away and think to myself that I’d like to try it sometime, then hear echoes of me saying the exact same thing last summer.  Except this time I know how to cook Mexican and would be willing to bet I can do just as good a job.

One of my favourite self-quotes of life advice from the last time I was here was “get rich and piss it all away.”  I obviously haven’t been doing the pissing part properly because I still have enough coin to last for multiple more years when I return to Amarillo in September.  In reality I’ve probably got one more year in me, do the complete loop through the Mississippi river delta, Florida keys and eastern seaboard then be done with it.  I might only make it one if I had to stay in Vancouver and deal with $7 pints and 12% sales tax on everything.  Goddam socialists.

And now there’s a new career path I could start investigating…professionally entering social media intern contests.  I mean in hindsight the Stampede one wasn’t that hard.  Make 1 minute video, vote, win.  Drink, ride horses, tweet about stuff.  Bam, done.  Everyone’s telling me to get the resume updated with that on there, and funnily enough it definitely counts as a job, although I wouldn’t call it working.  And if I felt the need to be sending my resume around Calgary (not gonna happen, don’t worry) I’d get all kinds of bonus street cred if only out of curiousity, whether being a social media intern was applicable to the job or not, at least it proves I’m a real person.

And it’s funny I still feel the need to validate that, perhaps now more than ever.  Blimpy Backgammon, human being.  For all the firebrand vitriol and enlighten thineself this that and the other thing, all I can really ever change is whether I’m sitting or standing, eating or drinking.  And right now I’m going to go sit in the sun and drink a beer.

You want my advice?  Do whatever the fuck you want all the goddam time, that’s all there is to it.
